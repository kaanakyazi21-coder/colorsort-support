Privacy Policy

Color Sort · Effective date: October 13, 2026 · Türkçe

Color Sort ("the game") is developed and published by YKM Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and the choices you have.

Summary

  • We do not collect any personal data. Nothing about you or how you play is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No accounts, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Information that stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, coins, collection, daily puzzle streak, settings and language choice are stored only on your device. The game works fully offline. Deleting the app removes this information.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Links

The Settings screen opens this page, the terms of use and your email app. These open outside the game; the game itself makes no network requests.

Ads and tracking

The current version shows no ads and does not track you. If a future version adds ads or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and on iPhone you will be asked for permission before any tracking.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, Brazil's LGPD, Turkey's KVKK, Indonesia's PDP Law, Thailand's PDPA or Malaysia's PDPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete data about you. Because the game does not send us any data, we hold nothing about you apart from emails you send us. For any request, email us at the address below.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page before they take effect.
